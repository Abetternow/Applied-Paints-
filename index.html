What We Do

Our Services

Exterior Painting

Protect and beautify your home's exterior with weather-resistant coatings tailored for Wellington's coastal climate. We prioritise thorough surface prep and premium coatings for long-lasting protection. Read More →

We begin with a detailed inspection and comprehensive surface preparation — cleaning, sanding, and repairing timber or metal as required. Our team selects premium exterior coatings chosen for UV and moisture resistance suited to Wellington's conditions, applying multiple coats for durability and an even finish. We protect your landscaping, windows and fixtures during work and manage scaffolding safely and professionally. The end result is a visually striking finish that boosts curb appeal and significantly extends the life of your exterior surfaces.

Interior Painting

Fresh, modern interiors tailored to your style — from bedrooms to kitchens, we deliver crisp, flawless finishes. We use low‑VOC paints and careful preparation to keep your home healthy and tidy. Read More →

Our interior process starts with protecting floors and furniture, followed by patching, sanding and priming as necessary to guarantee a perfect finish. We use high-quality, low‑VOC products and multiple carefully applied coats to achieve the desired look and durability. Attention to detail on trim, cornices and edges ensures clean lines, and we perform a thorough site clean so your home is ready to enjoy. Clients choose us for our craftsmanship, communication and commitment to leaving spaces better than we found them.

Wallpapering

Seamless wallpaper installation and removal for feature walls or full-room transformations, with precise pattern matching and clean edges. Our installers ensure a smooth, durable result every time. Read More →

We carefully remove old coverings (if required), prepare and level walls, and apply lining where necessary to create a perfect substrate. Our team matches patterns precisely, ensures tight seams and uses industry‑best pastes and techniques for a long-lasting installation. We work cleanly and efficiently to minimise disruption, and our finishing standards guarantee straight lines and a premium appearance. Whether you want a bold feature wall or a full-room redo, we deliver a showroom-quality result.

Roof Painting

Extend the life of your roof with specialist coatings that resist moss, mould and UV damage while improving appearance. Our roof systems protect and refresh your most exposed surfaces. Read More →

Roof work always starts with a careful safety plan and a thorough roof cleanse to remove moss, lichen and debris. We carry out necessary repairs and use primers and coatings designed for long-term protection against UV and moisture. Our application methods ensure even coverage and adhesion for a durable finish, and we inspect the result with you to ensure it meets expectations. Regular maintenance schedules can be provided to keep your roof performing and looking its best for years.

Timber Restoration

Restore timber decks, pergolas and weathered joinery to their original beauty with specialist cleaning, sanding and protective finishes. We match stains and finishes to complement your home. Read More →

Our timber restoration process begins with a careful inspection to identify rot, loose fixings or structural issues. We then cleanse, sand and, where necessary, replace damaged sections before applying premium stains or protective finishes formulated for NZ timber exposure. The result is renewed appearance and extended service life, with finishes that repel moisture and resist UV degradation. We also advise on maintenance to keep timber looking great season after season.

Fences & Gates

Boost curb appeal and protect boundaries with crisp, enduring fence and gate finishes tailored for local conditions. We use durable coatings that stand up to weather and wear. Read More →

We prepare fences and gates by cleaning, repairing and treating any rust or rot, then apply primers and finish coats suitable for timber or metal. Our approach balances durability with aesthetic appeal, using stains and paints that resist flaking and fading. Careful edge work and clean lines make a visible difference to a property's presentation, and our team works efficiently to minimise disruption. The finished result offers protection and a long-lasting, attractive appearance.
